Square feet and square yards both measure area. Flooring boxes are commonly labeled in square feet or square meters, while carpet may also be discussed in square yards. Correct conversion requires an area factor of nine—not a linear conversion applied to the final area incorrectly.

## The relationship between the units

One yard is three feet. A square yard is a square measuring three feet by three feet:

**3 ft × 3 ft = 9 sq ft**

Therefore:

- **1 sq yd = 9 sq ft**
- **Square yards = square feet ÷ 9**
- **Square feet = square yards × 9**

| Square feet | Square yards |
| ---: | ---: |
| 9 sq ft | 1 sq yd |
| 45 sq ft | 5 sq yd |
| 90 sq ft | 10 sq yd |
| 180 sq ft | 20 sq yd |
| 225 sq ft | 25 sq yd |

## Convert square feet to square yards

A room measuring 15 feet by 12 feet has:

**15 ft × 12 ft = 180 sq ft**

Convert the area:

**180 ÷ 9 = 20 sq yd**

Do not divide the room’s length and width by nine. If you want to convert each linear dimension to yards, divide each dimension by three: 15 feet is 5 yards and 12 feet is 4 yards, so 5 yards × 4 yards also equals 20 square yards.

## Convert square yards to square feet

For 25 square yards:

**25 × 9 = 225 sq ft**

This converts area only. It does not determine the number of carpet rolls, flooring boxes, or installation cuts.

## Square yards are not linear yards

A linear yard measures length. A square yard measures area. Carpet is cut from a roll with a fixed width, so a quoted linear length must be combined with the roll width to determine area.

For example, 5 linear yards of carpet from a 12-foot-wide roll means:

1. Convert length: 5 yd × 3 = 15 ft
2. Calculate roll area: 15 ft × 12 ft = 180 sq ft
3. Convert if needed: 180 ÷ 9 = 20 sq yd

Five linear yards from a 15-foot-wide roll would cover a different area. This is why “yards of carpet” is incomplete unless it is clear whether the number means square yards or a linear roll length with a stated width.

## Square meters

Metric flooring is generally described in square meters. Useful exact relationships include:

- **1 sq m = about 10.764 sq ft**
- **1 sq ft = 0.092903 sq m**
- **1 sq yd = 0.836127 sq m**

When possible, measure and calculate in the same system used on the product packaging. If conversion is necessary, keep full precision until the final display or whole-package calculation.

## Area does not replace carpet layout

Square-foot or square-yard area is useful for comparing quantities, but carpet is ordered and cut according to roll width, direction, seams, and sometimes pattern repeat. A 20-square-yard room may require more than 20 square yards cut from a roll if the room dimensions do not fit the roll efficiently.

Use [how to measure for carpet](/guides/how-to-measure-for-carpet) for a preliminary roll-width method. An installer should verify the final seam and cut layout.

## Common conversion mistakes

- Dividing square feet by three instead of nine
- Dividing both room dimensions by nine
- Treating a linear yard as a square yard
- Comparing a roll length with an area total
- Mixing square feet and square meters in one project sum
- Rounding a converted value before calculating packages
- Assuming converted room area is a purchase-ready carpet layout
